# Exhibition of Mastery

The Exhibition of Mastery is your opportunity to prove to yourself that you learned what you set out to learn. In the case of Jupyter Notebooks, it's time to create one from scratch for a purpose of your choosing. Ideally this is a project that serves some purpose, fills some gap in your workflow or organizational process. The Notebook should be usable by others, and perhaps produce report-like output.

The important point is to demonstrate that you feel confident enough in your Jupyter skills to use them for something real. If you can do that, you've accomplished your learning objectives.
